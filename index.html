Jianibieke Adalibieke

I am currently a researcher at Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Li Yi. I received my master's degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University, where I was advised by Prof. Fei Qiao. I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Mechanical Engineering and Automation at Beihang University.

My research focuses on developing generalizable dexterous policies for complex, long-horizon manipulation tasks in real-world scenarios.
